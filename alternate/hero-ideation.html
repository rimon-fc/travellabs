/ The homepage hero · ideation

Three ways to say what TravelLabs is in five seconds.

The current hero is a lovely painted coast, but it could belong to any travel brand. Each direction below is a working prototype, not a mock: scroll to it and it plays. All three keep the same headline and buttons, so you are judging the picture, not the words.

What the hero must sayAn AI lab solving travel's hardest problems: prices that move by the minute, one trip across many businesses, real money at stake.
What it must showAgents that act, wait and keep their word, with a person approving before money moves. ATFGraph, Keel and ATP, without a diagram.
What it must feel likeCurious, crafted and calm. A lab you trust with a real trip, not a hype reel.
Option A · inside the mcpx world

Painted by agents.

The painted scene stays, but now the agents paint it. Each small blue square is a job, like fares.search or hotels.search, laying down brush strokes inside a pencil sketch until the trip is complete. Then it stops and waits for you to approve.

Option B · mcpx, stretched to hero scale

The departures board.

Every traveller knows the clatter of a split-flap board. Here the headline flips in letter by letter. Then travel's hard problems arrive like departures, each one flipping from SEARCHING to its solved status. Every few seconds the board turns over to show the frameworks doing the work.

Option C · rebel, breaks the mcpx world

One brief, every moving part.

A traveller's brief appears, written in thousands of particles. It bursts into a living network: suppliers orbit an ATFGraph hub and agents stream between them. Every stream passes through the Keel gate and turns lime once checked. A person approves, and the particles settle into the headline. Move your cursor through it and the system makes way.

My pick

A for the homepage, C as a launch moment. Painted by agents keeps the craft of the site you already love, and adds the one thing the current hero is missing: agents doing real work, then waiting for a person. C is the strongest "frontier AI" statement. I'd use it for the January launch, a keynote or the Research page hero, where a deliberate break from the paper world earns its place. B is the safest high-impact option if you want the headline to land in under two seconds.
