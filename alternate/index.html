/ Our reason for being
/ Why travel

Travel is one of the hardest problems to solve.

It is inherently unpredictable, fragmented, complex and deeply interconnected.

  • Prices and availability change by the minute.
  • Millions of possibilities must be optimised.
  • One trip involves multiple businesses, systems and suppliers.
  • A single disruption can impact the entire journey.
  • Every traveller has unique needs and expectations.
  • Real money, customer trust and business profitability are at stake.

We research these fundamental problems, build open foundations and our own AI models, and turn them into new businesses, helping FCTG shape the future of travel.

/ Objectives

The hardest problems we are working to solve.

OBJECTIVE 01

Reimagine how travel is distributed.

An autonomous, agent-driven travel distribution ecosystem powered by agent-to-agent commerce, collaboration, autonomous decision-making, self-learning, self-healing and continuous optimisation.

OBJECTIVE 02

Unify global travel inventory.

Connect fragmented global travel supply into a universally accessible, interoperable and transactable ecosystem.

OBJECTIVE 03

Solve Ambient AI for travel.

Move from reactive AI to proactive intelligence and continuous autonomous assistance. Always-on, context-aware, self-learning intelligence that anticipates needs and acts on behalf of travellers and consultants.

OBJECTIVE 04

Make travel self-healing.

Anticipate, adapt to and autonomously resolve disruptions across the entire travel journey.

OBJECTIVE 05

Optimise and transact travel autonomously.

Autonomously construct optimal journeys, negotiate and transact safely, continuously optimising customer experience, pricing and profitability.
