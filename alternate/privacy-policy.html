Privacy Policy

Last updated 28 November 2026

This page explains the personal-information touchpoints on the TravelLabs research website. For the wider group’s handling of personal information, including rights, overseas disclosures and privacy contacts, read the Flight Centre Travel Group Privacy Notice.

Information you send

Our enquiry form asks for your name, email, organisation, topic and message. Our expression-of-interest form also accepts career information and a résumé. These submissions are sent through FormSubmit to rimon@travellabs.systems for the enquiry or expression of interest you choose to send. Please include only relevant information and avoid sending payment details or sensitive travel documents.

Website services

This version of the site is hosted on GitHub Pages and loads fonts from Google Fonts. Visiting it makes requests to these services. Their handling of technical request information is described in the GitHub Privacy Statement and Google Privacy Policy. FormSubmit’s submission checks are subject to its own privacy practices.

Your questions and choices

You can browse the research without submitting a form. To ask about information you have sent to TravelLabs, contact us. For privacy requests or concerns, the group Privacy Notice lists regional privacy contacts and explains the request and complaint process.
