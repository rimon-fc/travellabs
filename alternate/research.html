01 / Research

  
Autonomous Travel Distribution
Reimagine how travel is distributed.

  
01 / 05 · Discover

  

  
A traveller expresses an intent through a personal AI agent, rather than visiting a traditional travel website.

  
Travel reaches people wherever their journey begins.

  
Business impactNew distribution channels, new business models, greater market reach and reduced distribution friction.

  
Read all five stages
  1. Discover. A traveller expresses an intent through a personal AI agent, rather than visiting a traditional travel website.
  2. Connect. The agent discovers airline, hotel, cruise, touring and travel agency agents across the ecosystem.
  3. Collaborate. Agents exchange information, product offers, customer requirements and commercial terms.
  4. Transact. Agents negotiate, validate offers and securely complete authorised transactions.
  5. Evolve. The network learns from successful transactions, adapts to changing conditions, discovers new distribution opportunities and recovers from failed connections.





  
02 / Research

  
Universal Travel Inventory
Unify global travel supply.

  
01 / 05 · Discover

  

  
Reveal disconnected inventory sources across GDS, NDC, airlines, hotels, cruises, touring, activities and independent suppliers.

  
Thousands of suppliers. Different systems. One intelligent travel inventory.

  
Business impactGreater product choice, faster supplier integration, reduced connectivity complexity and new commercial opportunities.

  
Read all five stages
  1. Discover. Reveal disconnected inventory sources across GDS, NDC, airlines, hotels, cruises, touring, activities and independent suppliers.
  2. Understand. AI interprets different data formats, currencies, product descriptions, pricing structures and booking rules.
  3. Unify. Products are normalised, duplicates are resolved and relationships between products are established.
  4. Validate. Prices, availability, supplier access and booking conditions are continuously verified.
  5. Access. Travellers, consultants, developers and authorised AI agents can search, compare and transact through a unified interface.





  
04 / Research

  
Autonomous Disruption Management
Make travel self-healing.

  
01 / 05 · Monitor

  

  
The agent continuously monitors flights, weather, supplier updates and confirmed bookings.

  
The journey changes. The holiday continues.

  
Business impactReduced servicing costs, minimised financial losses, faster disruption recovery and greater customer trust.

  
Read all five stages
  1. Monitor. The agent continuously monitors flights, weather, supplier updates and confirmed bookings.
  2. Detect. A flight cancellation occurs. Connected hotel reservations, airport transfers and activities are immediately identified as being at risk.
  3. Reason. The agent evaluates alternative flights, available inventory, booking policies, traveller preferences and financial implications.
  4. Repair. Within authorised limits, the agent secures alternative flights, reschedules transfers, coordinates with hotels and rearranges affected activities.
  5. Protect. The recovered itinerary is validated, the traveller and consultant are informed, and continuous monitoring resumes.





  
05 / Research

  
Autonomous Travel Commerce & Optimisation
Find the best journey. Make it happen.

  
01 / 05 · Understand

  

  
The agent understands customer intent, preferences, budget, dates and travel constraints.

  
Millions of possibilities. One intelligently optimised journey.

  
Business impactHigher conversion, increased basket size, improved profitability and better customer experiences.

  
Read all five stages
  1. Understand. The agent understands customer intent, preferences, budget, dates and travel constraints.
  2. Explore. Thousands of flights, hotels, activities, destinations and transport combinations are discovered.
  3. Optimise. A neuro-symbolic optimisation engine eliminates infeasible combinations and evaluates the remaining possibilities against customer experience, cost, convenience, conversion and profitability.
  4. Transact. Agents negotiate offers, validate availability, obtain required approvals and securely execute bookings.
  5. Improve. The engine continuously monitors changing prices, inventory and customer needs, identifying better-value alternatives, relevant upgrades and commercial opportunities.
