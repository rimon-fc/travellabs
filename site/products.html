01 / Agentic TMS

Agents do the preparation.
Consultants make the call.

A workspace for agencies and travel management companies. Agents draft quotes, prepare bookings, check policy and organise changes. Consultants review the work and approve important actions.

  • Prepare options with the trip details and terms together.
  • Draft a change and explain what it affects.
  • Pause for approval before the agreed action.
  • Keep a record of decisions, bookings and outstanding work.

A conversation

03 / B2B AI

Connect once.
Meet the next generation
of travel agents.

Make an operator’s supported offers and actions available to compatible agents. Start with an existing system, or with documents and a human confirmation process.

  • Connect flights, stays, cars, extras and travel services.
  • Make capabilities and terms clear to every connected agent.
  • Manage searches within look-to-book limits.
  • Keep control of booking permission and travel payments.
