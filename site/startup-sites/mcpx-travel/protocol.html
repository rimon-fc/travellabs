/ The protocol

An open way for agents to book travel.

The Agentic Travel Protocol is a public spec for how agents search, hold, commit and look after trips. mcpx.travel is the network that runs it, on contracted supply.

/ Lifecycle

Four steps, every trip

Every booking follows the same path, so agents always know what is safe to do next.

01 · SEARCH

Ask in plain words

Agents send intent, not form fields. Results come back typed and ranked.

02 · HOLD

Keep it while they decide

Seats, rooms and slots are held with a clear expiry. Nothing is charged.

03 · COMMIT

All parts, or none

Every held part books together. If one fails, nothing is charged.

04 · LOOK AFTER

Events after booking

Delays, price drops and changes arrive as events with a fix to approve.


  





  

    
/ Principles
What the protocol promises

    
01
Intent, not forms
Agents describe what the traveller wants. The network works out the search.
02
Holds that expire
Every hold says how long it lasts and what it costs to keep.
03
All or nothing
Multi-part trips commit together. No half-booked holidays.
04
Certified endpoints
A supplier tool is checked before any agent can call it.
05
Every call recorded
Who asked, what was sent, what came back and why, kept for every booking.
06
Events after booking
Changes come back as events, so agents can help without polling.
