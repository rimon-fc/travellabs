    Agentic Travel Protocol · payments

    The booking rules. The spending permission. One connected purchase.

    The protocol ties each payment to the trip it pays for. The agent gets a controlled way to pay for the approved booking, inside the agreed amount and time.

    • Set the amount and the expiry from the approved trip.
    • Lock the card to that trip's suppliers.
    • Track the payment next to the booking, any change and any refund.
    • Or send the traveller a checkout link and let them pay.

    Card controls depend on the issuing programme. Cancelling a card does not reverse a payment that has already gone through.
