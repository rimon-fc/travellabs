/ Questions

Pricing questions

Why is there a search allowance?

Searching costs us money even when nobody books. Each booking includes 2,000 searches, which covers almost every agent. Extra searches past that are billed at a small flat rate each month.

When do I get paid for stays and experiences?

Earnings are paid at the end of each month, once the trip part is complete: after check-out or after the activity date. There is no minimum payout.

Can I add my own service fee?

Yes, when your business takes the payment. Show your fee separately from the travel price so the traveller can see both.

How do I get Scale pricing?

If you already sell travel or expect real volume, talk to us and we will put together a proposal.
