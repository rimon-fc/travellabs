The design builds on the Agentic Travel Framework: the parts that help an AI agent remember, act and follow your rules.

connect & act

ATF-Keel

Connects the agent to your travel tools, customer records and business rules. It gives the agent a way to do the work you allow.

remember & notice

ATFgraph

Keeps the customer’s story between tasks. A new enquiry, reply or price change can start the next piece of work.

a desk of its own

A private agent space

Each consultant’s agent is designed to work in its own protected space, with access limited to the accounts and information it needs.
