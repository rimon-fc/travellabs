Straight answers

What we help with.
What we can’t promise.

We help with the application.
The government decides.

We are not a government office. We cannot promise that a visa will be approved or that a traveller will be allowed to enter.

What if the traveller doesn’t need a visa?+

They may still need an entry form or another travel document. The check looks at the whole route, including stops, so you know what to prepare.

Does a real person check the forms?+

Yes. The AI agent prepares the paperwork. A trained person reviews each application before it is sent.

How long does a visa take?+

It depends on the country and the kind of visa. For a trip we can support, we’ll explain the expected wait. The government can take longer, so we do not promise an approval date.

Which trips can you help with?+

We’re starting with a small group of travel teams. The countries and visa types we can support are still being decided. We’ll confirm whether we can help with your trip before you decide to use the service.

What will it cost?+

Prices are still being worked out. Before you agree, you’ll see our service fee and any government fee listed separately.

What happens to passport details?+

Before we ask for any passport details, we’ll explain where they are kept, who can see them and when they are deleted. We’re still working out those details. Do not send passport scans through this page.
