The TravelLabs manifesto

Build agents that
earn the right to act.

We believe travel needs intelligence that understands its consequences.

The most useful agent is one people can trust with the next step. Our job is to make that trust easier to earn, easier to inspect and harder to lose.

01

Start with the person.

A booking affects where someone sleeps, how they get home and what they spend. Begin with that responsibility.

02

Earn permission to act.

Make the proposed purchase clear. Keep its limits attached to the work. Ask again when the important details change.

03

Show the work that matters.

Explain the choice, the terms and the next step. Keep a record people can follow when they need help.

04

Know how to wait.

A useful agent can pause for a person, a date or an event. It should remember enough to continue responsibly.

05

Treat failure as part of the design.

A connection will fail. A reply will arrive late. A room will become unavailable. Plan what happens next, including when a person must step in.

06

Build for the whole journey.

A trip continues after the payment. Keep watching supported changes and keep unfinished work visible.

07

Make room for every kind of operator.

A business with a rate sheet should have a path to agent distribution. Make its real capabilities and confirmation steps clear.

08

Learn in the open.

Write down the questions, the design choices and the limits. Use what the products teach us to improve the foundation.

Help put these beliefs to work.

Bring a real travel problem and a willingness to examine the hard parts.
