Company / TravelLabs

A lab that builds
what it learns.

TravelLabs brings research, a framework, a runtime and customer products into one place. The aim is simple: help travel trust agents with actions that matter.

Travel is a demanding place for an agent to work. Information changes. Several businesses may need to agree. A purchase has conditions. Someone still needs to get home.

We connect those practical problems to research and product work. The lab asks the questions, builds the foundation and brings it into the experiences people use.

Keel and atfgraph are part of that foundation. Agency, traveller and partner products put it to work.
