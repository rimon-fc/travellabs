Build with your preferred framework

atfgraph provides a managed runtime around agents. It also supports the factory work that turns a brief into checked software and agents.

Turn supply into usable tools

keel connects APIs and documents. It makes clear whether an operator supports a search, a request, a hold or an immediate confirmation.

Keep payment tied to the purchase

ATP provides Agentic Payment for Travel using a virtual credit card (VCC), with limits attached to the approved booking.

Create interfaces where people decide

Keel’s MCP-Apps extension gives compatible hosts interfaces for choices, trip details, approval and checkout.
