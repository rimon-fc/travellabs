THE FOUNDATIONS

Built for travel’s hardest parts.

Infrastructure for travel agents that can act, wait and keep looking after a trip. ATFGraph runs the work over time. Keel connects it to travel tools, transaction rules and controlled payments.

Durable jobs + graph server

ATFGraph

Beta

A durable runtime for agentic work.

Run agents built with any framework. Durable state preserves progress; temporal execution lets a job wait for an approval or booking update, then continue with its context.

Emitter compute
Schedules, incoming events and shared monitoring wake the agents that need to act.
Recovery & oversight
Recorded steps, retry rules, human approvals and shadow mode make long-running work inspectable.
WHY IT MATTERS IN TRAVEL

A disruption arrives after booking. The relevant agent wakes, retrieves the trip context and prepares the next action for approval.

Governed runtime + audit chain

Keel

Beta

Travel tools. ATP. Controlled payments.

Connect supply through checked MCP tools and MCP-Apps. ATP—the Agentic Travel Protocol—coordinates checking, reserving, booking, changing and cancelling across the parts of a trip.

Agentic Payment for Travel
Booking-linked virtual cards bound spending to the approved purchase, amount and expiry.
Supplier & transaction controls
Look-to-book protection, fresh price checks and permissions govern actions; booking and payment outcomes stay traceable.
WHY IT MATTERS IN TRAVEL

A trip spans several suppliers. ATP coordinates each supported step, records the outcome and routes failed bookings or payment exceptions for recovery.

      Agentic TMSResearch

      A personal agent that remembers each client, plans trips, books on approval and keeps watch after booking. Enquiries, changes and refunds keep moving; decisions that spend money wait for a person.

      Travel CanvasConcept

      A shared board for itineraries, stays and trade-offs. Keep the whole trip visible while consultants and travellers plan together.

      Voice AgentsUpcoming

      Answer everyday questions aloud, in more languages. Start with approved FAQs; hand unclear requests to a consultant. No booking changes.

      Proprietary travel modelsResearch roadmap

      Use consultant choices, preferences and booking outcomes to improve ranking, recommendations and the next best step.

      Agentic paymentsResearch

      Tie each payment to the traveller’s permission, the booking and a clear record of what happened.

      Voice & ConciergeIncubating

      Explore a voice and messaging concierge for travel businesses. Separate from the planned IQR Voice Agents capability.
