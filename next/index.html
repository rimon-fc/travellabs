Our reason for being

We’re building the intelligence layer for travel.

TravelLabs is the research and development division of Flight Centre Travel Group.

AI agents are starting to plan, buy and look after travel for people. Travel is the hardest place for them to do it well: prices change by the minute, one trip involves many businesses, and real money is at stake.

We research these problems, build open foundations and our own models, and turn them into businesses, so FCTG helps decide how travel is sold next.

Our five objectives

Five objectives. The systems we’re working towards.

  1. Reimagine how travel is distributed.

    A trusted way for AI agents and suppliers to do business, so travel reaches people wherever their trip begins.

  2. Make agentic commerce safe.

    Agents that people and businesses can trust with a real trip and real money.

  3. Give agents a sense of time and a word they keep.

    We’re building agents that remember the trip, act within agreed terms and keep working until the traveller is home.

  4. Open travel to everyone.

    Every builder can sell it, every traveller can get a better deal, and every supplier, large or small, can be found.

  5. Turn FCTG’s expertise into intelligence.

    Decades of knowing what makes a great trip, built into our own models and an agent for every consultant.
