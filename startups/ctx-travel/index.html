what ctx means

ctx is short for context.

Context is everything that makes a trip yours: who's going, what you can spend, what you've saved, what you've already booked, and where you'll be each day. Most travel apps forget all of it the moment you close them. ctx keeps it, so every answer fits you.

questions

Fair questions

What does ctx stand for?

Context. It means ctx remembers the things that make your trip yours, like who's going, your budget and your bookings, so its answers fit you instead of everyone.

How can member prices be lower than public sites?

Some airlines and hotels keep their best prices for members only, and share them through travel companies they have deals with. Flight Centre Travel Group has those deals. Joining ctx makes you a member. You always see the normal price next to yours.

So how does ctx make money?

Hotels and airlines pay ctx a small fee when you book, the same way they pay other travel companies. On price-drop refunds we keep a small share of the saving, and we always show that share before anything changes.

Will ctx ever book without asking me?

No. ctx searches and suggests on its own, but every booking, payment and change waits for your yes. Price-drop rebooks follow the rules you agreed to when you switched them on, and you're told every time one happens.

Does the refund work on bookings I made somewhere else?

Yes, for hotels. Upload the confirmation and ctx tracks that exact stay. If the hotel stops selling the room, or free cancellation has passed, there is nothing to act on, and we'll tell you so.

What happens when a flight gets cheaper?

If the airline allows it, ctx asks the airline to reprice your ticket and the difference comes back as airline credit. Your flight, seats and booking reference stay exactly the same.

What is a stay upgrade?

After you book, ctx keeps an eye on better-rated stays nearby. If one costs the same or less for your dates, it suggests the swap. Nothing changes unless you say yes.

Why no coupons or promo codes?

Coupons give a lower price to some people, some of the time. Members simply see the private price, every time, and ctx keeps working on it after you book.

Will my room change when you rebook?

Never. We only rebook when the same hotel, the same room type and the same dates are available for less. Otherwise your original booking stays exactly as it is.

When does the money reach me?

We start the refund as soon as the cheaper booking is confirmed. It usually reaches your card or account within a few working days, depending on your bank.

What happens to the files I upload?

We read the hotel, dates, room and price so we can track the stay, and nothing else. Files stay private to your account and you can delete them at any time.
