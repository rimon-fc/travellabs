How it works · part one

The Network does four jobs

These are the jobs nobody does today. Each one is done once, by the Network, for every member.

A few lights hold steady: members everyone can check.

1
Checks who you are

Every agent and supplier is checked before joining. An agent carries an ID that says who it works for and what it may do: look, hold or book.

2
Sets one way of doing business

The same steps for everyone: ask, offer, negotiate, hold, book, change, cancel. Build it once and you can deal with every member.

3
Moves the money

The Network takes payment from the buyer and pays the supplier. Suppliers know they will be paid. Buyers only pay for what was confirmed.

4
Keeps the record and settles problems

Every request, offer and agreement is written down. If something goes wrong, both sides look at the same record, and there is one agreed way to sort it out.

Who it is for

Two kinds of members. Both get something on day one.

The Network only works when both sides join. So it has to be worth it for each of them.

If you sell travel

Airlines, hotels, wholesalers, tour and activity companies

  • Requests only from checked agents, and you can see who they book for.
  • Answer with a private offer, or negotiate inside the limits you set.
  • Get paid through the Network for every confirmed booking.
  • One set of rules, instead of a separate deal with every agent.

You stay in charge of which agents you answer, what price each one sees and your lowest price.

Join as a supplier
If you build AI agents

AI assistants, travel agencies, business travel teams, online travel sites

  • One way in to many suppliers, instead of one deal at a time.
  • An ID that suppliers recognise, so you don't get blocked.
  • Private offers and prices you can negotiate for your traveller.
  • Payment and problems handled by the Network.

You stay in charge of your traveller's budget, the rules your agent follows and when a person must approve.
