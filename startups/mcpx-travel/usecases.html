What you can build

Travel has endless shapes. Here are a few teams are building on mcpx.travel.

SEARCH
Personal · 01

Weekend escapes

Pick a city and a budget. Get three weekends away, priced door to door, ready to hold.

  1. Traveller sets a city and a budget
  2. Agent searches flights and stays together
  3. Three trips come back with total prices
  4. One tap holds the favourite
DISCOVER
Personal · 02

Swipe to choose

Swipe through priced trip ideas. The one you keep is held while you check with friends.

  1. Pick a mood, dates and budget
  2. Swipe through real, priced ideas
  3. The keeper is held with an expiry
  4. Book it when everyone says yes
WEATHER
Personal · 03

Rainy day rescue

When rain cancels a boat tour, the agent finds an indoor museum slot the same day and swaps the booking.

  1. The operator cancels for weather
  2. Agent finds open museum and food tours nearby
  3. Traveller picks one in the chat
  4. Refund and new booking land together
TEAM
Business · 04

Team trip in team chat

Six people to a conference, booked from one thread, on one invoice, inside policy.

  1. Organiser asks in the team channel
  2. Agent finds flights for each person
  3. Manager approves in the thread
  4. Everything books together on one invoice
CREATOR
Business · 05

Creator trip drops

A travel creator sells a small group trip in their DMs. Spaces are held as people say yes.

  1. Creator posts the trip
  2. Followers reply in DMs
  3. Agent holds a space for each yes
  4. Each person pays with their own link
SAVINGS
Business · 06

Policy-smart booking

The agent checks company policy and finds the same trip for less, with the saving shown.

  1. Employee asks for a trip
  2. Agent reads the travel policy
  3. Finds an in-policy option for less
  4. Books after approval and logs the saving
