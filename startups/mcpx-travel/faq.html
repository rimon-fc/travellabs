Getting started01

What is mcpx.travel?

mcpx.travel is built by Rimon R for Travellabs.

mcpx.travel lets AI agents and assistants search, hold, commit and look after flights, hotels and experiences through one connection. You build the experience. We run the travel layer underneath.

How do I connect?

Through our MCP server, REST API or CLI. The quickstart walks through getting a key and running your first search.

What can my agent book?

Flights from 600+ airlines covering 99% of passenger volume, including 100 airlines with negotiated fares. 2.9 million properties in one feed. 200,000 experiences in 10,000 destinations.

What do I need for real bookings?

A live API key, your integration pointed at the live environment, and a payment method set up. Pay-as-you-go access does not need a negotiated contract.

Do I need my own travel licence?

No. You can sell under our accreditation. If you already hold your own, you can use it on the Scale plan.

Pricing and earnings02

What does it cost?

Flights carry a flat fee per ticket. Stays and experiences are free to book and earn you a share. See pricing for the details.

How do I earn?

You earn a share on every completed stay and experience your agent books. Your share grows with your volume.

When am I paid?

At the end of each month, with no minimum payout, once each trip part is complete.

Can I charge my own fee?

Yes, when your business takes the payment. Show it separately from the travel price.

Payments and support03

Who takes the payment?

We can. The agent pays with a virtual card locked to the approved trip, or sends the traveller a checkout link.

Or you can. Take payment yourself and keep a prepaid balance with us. Bookings are drawn from that balance.

Does the agent ever see card numbers?

No. The agent pays with a virtual card that only works for the approved trip, inside its amount and time limit. The traveller approves the trip first.

Who helps if something goes wrong?

Our team supports travellers 24/7. Travellers can keep talking to your agent, and we step in behind it when a person is needed.

The protocol04

What is the Agentic Travel Protocol?

An open spec for how agents search, hold, commit and look after trips. Read the overview on the protocol page.

What does hold, then commit mean?

Your agent can hold seats, rooms and slots while the traveller decides. When they say yes, every held part is booked together. If one part fails, none of it is charged.

Is the protocol tied to mcpx.travel?

No. The spec is public. mcpx.travel is a network that runs it, with contracted supply behind it.
