Manifesto

Meet Trunk.Always a little curious.

Let your work speak for itself.

Whatever you choose to do, be so good at it that your work speaks for itself.

Follow your curiosity deeply.

Read deeply about what you love and are passionate about. Pursue knowledge by any means necessary, even if your methods look strange or unconventional to the rest of the world.

Bring your vision to life.

Move with speed, act with control, and wait with patience. All that matters is bringing your vision to life.

We are impact players.

Impact players do not merely do the job they were given. They figure out the job that actually needs doing.

Put friction in the right places.

Remove friction from the right behaviours and deliberately add friction to the wrong ones.

Make failure intelligent.

Failure is intelligent when it occurs in genuinely uncertain territory, is hypothesis-driven, and is no larger than necessary to learn.
